# Student video projects

For the following projects, please note that the teacher provided students with extra time to add
voice-over to their video projects. 

Our project did not support adding human voices to video projects.

To reproduce the original videos as closely as possible, the audio was AI-generated to protect the identities of the children involved.
Also, all identifying information has been removed from the links.

For the following links, make sure to turn on the audio. 
You can enable audio by clicking on the speaker icon in the lower-right part of the screen.
